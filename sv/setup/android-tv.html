Installera TVMaster on Android TV & NVIDIA Shield

Deploy TVMaster on Google TV, NVIDIA Shield, Xiaomi, and Mecool boxes in minuter. This guide uses TiviMate Premium for its slick grid EPG, client-side DVR, and multi-user profiles—ideal for households streaming live Premier League, LaLiga, NFL, and Asian channels together.

Installation time: 10 minuter · Skill: Intermediate Requirements: Google account, TVMaster portal URL, optional TiviMate Premium sub Ideal for: 4K HDR sports, DVR recordings, multi-profile households

Why Android TV customers stay longer

  • Native 4K60 playback with Dolby Vision on NVIDIA Shield Pro and Chromecast with Google TV.
  • TiviMate Premium enables 10 recording slots, series auto-record, and cloud backup of channel groups.
  • Voice search across TVMaster live channels, catch-up, and VOD libraries using Google Assistant.
  • Perfect companion for sports fans switching between EPL, Serie A, NFL RedZone, and beIN MENA feeds.

Installera TiviMate & connect TVMaster

  1. Ladda ner TiviMate – Open Google Play on your Android TV, search for TiviMate IPTV Player, and install.
  2. Launch and grant storage – On first open, allow Storage permission so TiviMate can cache EPG data and recordings.
  3. Add TVMaster playlist – Choose Add playlist ▸ Xtream Codes, then paste the TVMaster portal URL, username, and password from your welcome email.
  4. Sync channel database – After login, allow the initial sync (30K channels, 80K VOD). Enable Auto Update and Update on app launch.
  5. Create channel groups – Press the menu button, choose Manage groups, favourite Premier League, US Sports, Thai, Filipino, or regional packs your household uses most.
  6. Activate TiviMate Premium – Purchase via in-app billing to unlock DVR, multi-EPG, and backup. Sign in with your TiviMate account so each device syncs layouts.

Optimise playback & DVR

Enable timeshift: Settings ▸ Playback ▸ Timeshift buffer: choose Auto and allocate 2 GB for pausing live matches.

Record overnight fights: In the program guide, long-press any UFC/boxing event and tap Record Series so replays land in the DVR folder.

Backup layout: Go to Settings ▸ Backups to store your channel order in Google Drive. Perfect for clients swapping devices.

Troubleshooting

Playback failed? Switch decoder to Software for problematic channels, or enable Buffer size: 12 seconds.

No catch-up tile? Ensure the playlist is set to Xtream Codes login, not M3U-only. Catch-up populates automatically for eligible channels.

Shield remote lag? Clear TiviMate cache, update to Shield Experience 9+, and pair the remote via Bluetooth instead of IR.
